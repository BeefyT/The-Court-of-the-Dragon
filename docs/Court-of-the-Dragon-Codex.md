# THE COURT OF THE DRAGON
### A Warband for Trench Crusade

---

## THE DAMNED OF WALLACHIA

In the year 1573, when Byzantium had fallen and the Infernal advance crept north, the Sacred Order of the Dragon held the line in the hills of Wallachia. A million Heretics were impaled in those hills, and Heaven called it a miracle.

It was no miracle. It was a bargain.

The knights of the Order had gone to the Scholomance — the Devil's school hidden beneath the Carpathian peaks — and struck a pact for power enough to hold. Immortality. Blood-sorcery. The strength to drink the war itself. They took the loan meaning never to repay it: to turn Hell's own gift against Hell's own servants, and to keep the borrowed eternity for themselves.

Heaven named them apostates and shut its gates. Hell, whose every law is written in contracts and collateral, named them thieves — for in the theology of the Pit there is no sin worse than welshing on a debt. So the Court belongs to neither power. They are hunted by the angels they betrayed and the devils they cheated, and they answer to no master at all.

They answer only to the blood. For the power the Order stole was never truly theirs — they drank it, long ago, from three things older and hungrier that still sleep in the crypts beneath Castle Dracula. The Voivode rules the Court, deadliest of all his kind; yet even he is a child beside the Elders downstairs, from whose veins his own gift first came.

Now they march to the great war in greatcoats and grave-dust, a handful of immortal predators trailing a herd of the dominated and the bled. They are slow. They are few. And the longer the killing lasts, the stronger they become.

## SPECIAL RULES

These rules apply to every Court of the Dragon Warband.

### SATED MARKERS

The Court's power is measured in SATED MARKERS, blood harvested from others, kept entirely separate from BLOOD MARKERS.

BLOOD MARKERS on a Court model function as normal: your opponent may spend them against your rolls — they are a weakness. SATED MARKERS are the Court's strength, fuelling its states and abilities. A model can hold a maximum of 6 Sated Markers. Track them with a distinct counter.

The Feed ACTION: a model with the FEED keyword may take a Feed ACTION against an enemy model, or a friendly non-VAMPIRE model, within 1" that has no BLESSING MARKERS and does not have the ARTIFICIAL keyword, then take a Risky Success Roll. On a Success, place 1 BLOOD MARKER on that model and the feeding model gains 1 SATED MARKER. Feed is a Risky action — a failure ends the Activation — so it is usually taken last.

### THE STATE LADDER

Every vampire model checks its Sated total to determine its state: Base (0-2 Sated), Ascended (3-4 Sated), Blood-Crazed (5-6 Sated).

A model's state is locked at the start of its Activation and does not change until the start of its next Activation, even if it gains or spends Sated in between. A model may use only one ability that costs Sated per Activation.

### BLOOD-CRAZED BEHAVIOUR

A Blood-Crazed model may take no ACTION other than Move, Dash, Charge or Fight, and must take a Charge ACTION if it is able to. Which model it must Charge is determined by The Leash.

### THE LEASH

Each time a friendly Blood-Crazed model is Activated, check for a friendly DOMINION model within 6". In range: it must Charge the nearest enemy model. Not in range: it must Charge the nearest model, friend or foe. While no friendly DOMINION model is on the battlefield, every friendly Blood-Crazed model is loose and must Charge the nearest model, friend or foe.

### UNDEAD FORTITUDE

Court vampires do not bleed out as mortals do. A model with the VAMPIRE keyword relies on REGENERATE rather than on medical treatment: it cannot benefit from a Medi-kit, and no Treat ACTION may be carried out on it.

### THE BETRAYED CHURCH

The Court are apostates. Models attacking a Court model gain +1 INJURY DICE if they have one or more BLESSING MARKERS.

### GRAVE-LAW

A model may reform (return from Out of Action) no more than once per game, regardless of source.

### ARMOURY RESTRICTIONS

The Court may not take FIRE weapons, and no Court model may wear Machine Armour — the Order predates the machine, and dead flesh will not be bolted into it. Effects that would grant Machine Armour grant nothing.

## NEW KEYWORDS

**DRAGON COURT** — This model is part of the Court of the Dragon faction.

**FEED** — A model with the FEED keyword may take the Feed ACTION (see Sated Markers) to harvest Sated from a valid target.

**VAMPIRE** — A deathless lord of the Court. VAMPIRE models use Sated Markers and the State Ladder, and cannot be targeted by their own faction's Feed ACTION.

**DOMINION** — A model with the DOMINION keyword holds the Leash. Friendly Blood-Crazed models within 6" of a DOMINION model must Charge the nearest enemy model rather than the nearest model of any kind.

## THE BLOODLINES

The Court's power flows from three Elders sleeping in the crypts beneath Castle Dracula — the things the Voivode himself first drank from.

**Pledge.** At Warband creation, each **ELITE** vampire pledges to **one** Elder as its origin blood. This is per-model: a Warband may field a Mara Voivode beside a Cazimir Boyar. Origin blood is fixed and never changes. Pledging grants access to that Elder's **Elder Blood** purchase.

**Elder Blood** — bought with ducats at Warband creation. A vampire may take **1** Elder Blood gift, from its pledged Elder only.

**Bloodline Ladders** — earned in campaign. Every Elder has a three-rung ladder. An ELITE vampire may climb **any** Elder's ladder, not only its pledged one — power pulls the blood. Each ladder is climbed **bottom-up**: a rung may be taken only if the rung below it on that ladder is already held.

**Advancement — choose, don't roll.** When a Court ELITE vampire would make an Advancement Roll, it does not roll on the standard Skill Tables. Instead, choose any one Bloodline rung the model is currently eligible for and the model learns it.

### MARA, THE RED FAMINE

*The eldest and hungriest. Her blood is appetite; it climbs by killing and drinking, and it pays the whole Court back.*

**Elder Blood**

- **Glutton for Blood** *(25👑)* — its Feed ACTION grants 2 Sated instead of 1.

**Ladder**

1. **Sate the Kill** — when this model takes an enemy Out of Action with a Melee Attack, it gains 1 Sated (no action, no roll).
2. **Shared Feast** *(needs Sate the Kill)* — whenever this model gains Sated from Feeding or from Sate the Kill, one friendly VAMPIRE within 6" also gains 1 Sated. Once per Activation.
3. ★ **The Red Famine** *(needs Shared Feast)* — while Ascended, its Melee Attacks gain +1 INJURY DICE against any model carrying 1 or more BLOOD MARKERS.

### NEGRU VODĂ, THE THRONE OF THORNS

*The Black Voivode who ruled these mountains first. His blood is rulership — this ladder grows by holding the leash.*

**Elder Blood**

- **Crown of Dread** *(25👑)* — −1 DICE to all Success Rolls for enemy models within 6". With the bearer's FEAR, melee attacks against it suffer −2 in total. A model cannot suffer this penalty from more than one Crown of Dread.

**Ladder**

1. **Seat of Command** — gains the DOMINION keyword.
2. **The Driven Hunt** *(needs Seat of Command)* — friendly Blood-Crazed models within this model's Leash add +1 DICE to their Melee Attacks.
3. ★ **The Throne of Thorns** *(needs The Driven Hunt)* — enemies treat the area within 6" of this model as Difficult terrain, and while this model is on the battlefield and not Down, your Warband ignores the Shaken rule.

### CAZIMIR, THE HUNDRED-GRAVED

*Buried a hundred times, risen from every grave. His blood is the curse that will not let the body rest. It climbs by refusing to die.*

**Elder Blood**

- **Unhallowed Flesh** *(25👑)* — permanent −1 INJURY MODIFIER, which stacks with worn armour.

**Ladder**

1. **Knit the Wound** — its REGENERATE value rises by 1.
2. **Rise from the Mire** *(needs Knit the Wound)* — the first time it is taken Down each game, it immediately stands and may move 3". This does not trigger on a Grave-Law reform.
3. ★ **The Hundred Graves** *(needs Rise from the Mire)* — the first time this model would be taken Out of Action each game, it is instead removed and reforms at a friendly board edge at the start of your next Turn with 2 Sated. This does not trigger if the wound was caused by a FIRE or BLESSED attack.

## WARBAND CREATION

- A starting Warband has **700 ducats (👑)**.
- It must include exactly one **Voivode**, who is its Leader.
- It may include no more than **6 models with the ELITE keyword**.
- Each ELITE vampire pledges to one Elder Bloodline (Mara, Negru Vodă, or Cazimir) when it is recruited, and may buy 1 Elder Blood gift from that Elder.
- **Limited Potential:** the Shrike and the Leech cannot exceed 7 Experience Points, even if Promoted to ELITE.
- Mercenaries are hired with Glory (☼) and follow **Neither Shepherd Nor Wolf**.
- Glory Items are purchased with Glory (☼); see **Glory Items**.
- All other limits follow the standard rules of *Trench Crusade*.

## CAMPAIGN PLAY — THE LONG HUNGER

This replaces the Trauma Step for a Court Warband. All other Campaign Phase steps are unchanged. The herd — the Bled and the Strix — are mortal and live and die by the standard rules. All rates in this rule are provisional.

The Thirst: an ELITE vampire never rolls on the Trauma Table and never gains Battle Scars. Instead, in the Trauma Step, if it was taken Out of Action during the game it gains 1 Thirst Marker. 1 Thirst — Fraying: it becomes Blood-Crazed at 4+ Sated instead of 5+. 2 Thirst — Slipping: halve its Leash ranges, both projected and received. 3 Thirst — Lost: replace its entry on your Warband Roster with a Shrike; its Experience and Skills are gone and its Battlekit moves to your Arsenal.

If the Voivode is Lost, promote one Boyar to Voivode — keeping its Experience, Skills and any Battlekit it can legally carry — or recruit a new Voivode at the Quartermaster Step.

Torpor: the Shrike and the Leech make Survival Rolls as normal Troops, but on a 1-2 they are not removed from your roster — they enter Torpor and miss the next battle.

The Vintage: when your Warband collects Loot in the Exploration Step, you may take any portion of it as Vintage instead of ducats, at 10 ducats = 1 Vintage. Vintage cannot be spent on Battlekit, Glory Items or recruits. In the Quartermaster Step you may spend 10 Vintage to remove 1 Thirst Marker from one vampire, no more than once per Campaign Phase.
